# Privacy Policy — ReceiptStacker

Effective date: 2026-02-15

ReceiptStacker ("App", "we", "our", "us") is a receipt and expense management mobile app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Summary

- ReceiptStacker is designed to be local-first.
- Receipt and budgeting data is stored on your device.
- We do not run an advertising network inside the app.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- Data leaves your device only when you explicitly choose actions such as export/share/backup/restore.

## 2. Information you provide

You may provide:
- Receipt images
- Receipt details (merchant, date, amount, categories, notes, items)
- Budget and category settings
- Optional support emails you send us

## 3. How data is stored

ReceiptStacker stores app data locally on your device storage. We do not operate a proprietary cloud database for your receipts.

## 4. Permissions and why they are used

- Camera: scan paper receipts
- Photo Library / Media: import receipt images and optional save/share flows
- Biometrics (Face ID / fingerprint): optional local app unlock/authentication
- Background fetch / boot / wake lock (platform-dependent): optional scheduled backup tasks configured by the user

Permissions are requested only to support these features.

## 5. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent user data.

Data may be transferred outside your device only when you initiate it, for example:
- Exporting reports/files
- Sharing files through another app
- Restoring from a file source you selected
- Optional cloud backup flows you explicitly enable

If you use third-party services (email, cloud storage, messaging apps), their privacy policies apply to the data you share through them.

## 6. Tracking, analytics, and advertising

ReceiptStacker is intended to operate without ad tracking SDKs.

If this changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before or at release.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored locally, you control retention by:
- Deleting records in the app
- Clearing app data
- Uninstalling the app

Exported or shared copies are controlled by you and/or the third-party services you choose.

## 8. Security

We rely on platform protections and best-effort safeguards, including:
- OS-level sandboxing and storage controls
- Optional biometric lock features
- Secure network transport where network features are used

No method of storage/transmission is guaranteed 100% secure.

## 9. Children’s privacy

ReceiptStacker is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-hosted backend.

## 10. International users

If you choose to export/share data through third-party services, data may be processed in other countries according to those services’ terms.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version date appears at the top.
